CallBridge for Windows

Know who's calling, and what's open for them, before you say hello.

CallBridge is a desktop softphone built for MSP technicians. When a customer calls, it shows their company and open ConnectWise tickets, lets you take notes straight onto a ticket, and drafts your time entry when you hang up.

Quick start

From download to your first call

Your admin does steps 2 to 4 once per computer. Technicians only need step 5 and a headset.

  1. InstallDownload the MSI from the latest release and approve the Windows prompt.
  2. Connect the phoneSettings → Phone account and SIP. Enter your extension and SIP login, save, then Register.
  3. Connect ConnectWiseSettings → ConnectWise. Choose PSA or Platform, enter the keys, and test.
  4. Load customersSync ConnectWise contacts so callers are recognized.
  5. Set your headset and member IDSettings → Audio and Sign-in. Then take a call.
